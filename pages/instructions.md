# How to play

- You are dropped into a random place. Look around and work out where you are.
- Open the map in the corner, drop a pin, then press Guess.
- The closer you are, the more points you earn, up to 5,000 per round.
- Timed and Blitz modes add a countdown. If time runs out your pin is submitted automatically.
- Use a room code to play the same places as your friends, or try the daily challenge.
